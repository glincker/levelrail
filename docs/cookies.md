# Cookie notice

Last updated: 2026-10-05.

## Cookies

The Levelrail website's own code does not set cookies, so there is no cookie banner to dismiss.

## Local storage

The site saves your light or dark theme choice in your browser's local storage so it persists between visits. It is not sent to us and holds no personal information. You can clear it from your browser settings at any time.

## Third parties

If you follow a link to another site, such as GitHub or Discord, that site may set its own cookies under its own policy.
