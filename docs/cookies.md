Last updated: 2026-11-20.

This notice covers the website only, not the Levelrail software. The product sends no usage telemetry.

## Display choices

The site saves your theme, background and motion choices in your browser's local storage so they persist between visits. They stay in your browser and are not sent to us.

## Analytics

The site loads [PostHog](https://posthog.com) to count visits and catch page errors. The browser may keep an identifier in cookies or local storage so a return visit can be recognized. We do not run advertising trackers and we do not sell personal data.

Use the opt-out on the [website privacy page](https://levelrail.com/privacy/#out-h) to stop capture from this browser, or block the script with a content blocker.

## Third parties

If you follow a link to another site, such as GitHub or Discord, that site may set its own cookies under its own policy.

## Clearing it

You can delete cookies and local storage for this site at any time from your browser settings. Doing so also resets the analytics opt-out, so set it again afterwards.
