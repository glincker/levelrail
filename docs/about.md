**Levelrail is built by GLINCKER, in the open**

A self-hosted deployment platform for your own Linux servers. It is Apache 2.0 licensed and developed in public on GitHub.

[View on GitHub](https://github.com/glincker/levelrail) | [Roadmap](/roadmap)

## Who builds it

Levelrail is a GLINCKER project. The source, issues and discussions live at github.com/glincker/levelrail.

## License

Apache 2.0, one edition, no paywalled features. See Pricing for what that means in practice.

## Honest status

Levelrail is pre-release software. The roadmap and feature status pages say what is shipped, what is experimental and what is not proven yet, and the changelog lists every release.
