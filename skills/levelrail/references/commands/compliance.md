# levelrail-cli compliance

Generated by `levelrail-cli docs`. Do not edit.

## levelrail-cli compliance

Subcommands: access-review, evidence

```
Usage:
  levelrail-cli compliance evidence create --from DATE --to DATE [--out FILE] [--no-wait]   build a signed evidence pack and download it
  levelrail-cli compliance evidence list [flags]                                          list evidence packs
  levelrail-cli compliance evidence download <id> [--out FILE] [flags]                    download a finished pack
  levelrail-cli compliance evidence verify <id> [flags]                                   re-check a stored pack against its manifest and signature
  levelrail-cli compliance access-review start [--name N] [--due-days D] [--repeat-days R]  open a review of every user, membership, token and grant
  levelrail-cli compliance access-review list [flags]                                     list review campaigns
  levelrail-cli compliance access-review show <id> [flags]                                show a campaign and its rows
  levelrail-cli compliance access-review keep|revoke <id> <item-id>... [flags]            record a decision per row
  levelrail-cli compliance access-review complete <id> [flags]                            apply revocations and close the campaign

Evidence packs are records for an auditor to examine. They are not a certification.
```
