## levelrail-cli session-policy

Subcommands: clear, effective, list, set

```
Usage:
  levelrail-cli session-policy list                         show every team's session policy
  levelrail-cli session-policy set <team-id> [--idle 30m] [--max-age 12h] [--allow CIDR ...] [--require-mfa]
                                                    [--max-sessions N] [--on-limit evict_oldest|reject]
                                                    replace a team's policy (omitted values turn that rule off)
  levelrail-cli session-policy clear <team-id>              remove a team's policy
  levelrail-cli session-policy effective                    show the policy that applies to you and your address

Locked out by an IP allowlist? On the host run: levelrail access-policy-reset --yes
```
