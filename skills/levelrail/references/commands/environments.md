## levelrail-cli environments

Subcommands: create, delete, list, set-default, update

```
Usage:
  levelrail-cli environments list [flags]                       list every environment with its kind and app counts
  levelrail-cli environments create --name NAME [--kind KIND] [--protected] [--sort-order N] [flags]
  levelrail-cli environments update <id> [--name NAME] [--kind KIND] [--sort-order N] [--protected=true|false] [flags]
  levelrail-cli environments delete <id> [--move-to ID] [flags]
  levelrail-cli environments set-default <project-id> <env-id|name> [flags]   make an environment its project's default

KIND is one of dev, test, uat, production, custom. The built-in
environments (env_dev, env_test, env_uat, env_production) cannot be
deleted. Deleting an environment that still holds apps or databases needs
--move-to ID; a protected environment cannot take part in that bulk move.
Move one app with "levelrail-cli apps move-env" and one database with
"levelrail-cli databases move-env".
```
