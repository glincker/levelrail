## levelrail-cli teams

Subcommands: avatar, create, delete, events, invites, list, members, rename, roles, set-description, set-slug, show, transfer, use

```
Usage:
  levelrail-cli teams list                                  teams you belong to, with your role
  levelrail-cli teams create <name>                         create a team (instance admins); you become its owner
  levelrail-cli teams show <team>                           one team and your role in it
  levelrail-cli teams rename <team> <name>                  rename a team (owners)
  levelrail-cli teams delete <team>                         delete an empty team (owners)
  levelrail-cli teams use [<team>] [--project ID]           save the team and project every command is scoped to; no team clears it
  levelrail-cli teams roles                                 the four team roles and what each grants
  levelrail-cli teams transfer <team> <user-id>             hand ownership to another member; you become admin
  levelrail-cli teams events <team> [--limit N]             the team's membership audit trail (admins)
  levelrail-cli teams set-slug|set-description|avatar ...   same as "orgs" (profile fields and avatar)
  levelrail-cli teams members <verb> ...                    list, set-role, remove, leave
  levelrail-cli teams invites <verb> ...                    list, create, revoke, accept

Every command also takes --team ID (and, before the command name, --project ID)
to narrow what the control plane shows, overriding APP_TEAM, APP_PROJECT and the saved context.
```

## levelrail-cli teams avatar

Subcommands: rm, set

```
levelrail-cli: unknown orgs avatar subcommand "--help"

Usage:
  levelrail-cli orgs show <org> [--json]                   profile, counts, failing apps and recent activity
  levelrail-cli orgs rename <org> <name>                   change the display name (org admins)
  levelrail-cli orgs set-slug <org> <slug>                 3 to 40 chars: lowercase letters, digits, single hyphens; "" clears
  levelrail-cli orgs set-description <org> <text>          at most 500 characters; "" clears
  levelrail-cli orgs avatar set <org> <file>               upload a PNG or JPEG avatar
  levelrail-cli orgs avatar rm <org> [--yes]               remove the avatar

"teams" accepts the same verbs. Every command takes --token, --api-url, --profile and --json.
```

## levelrail-cli teams members

Subcommands: leave, list, remove, set-role

```
Usage:
  levelrail-cli members list <team>                        members and their roles
  levelrail-cli members set-role <team> <user-id> <role>   owner, admin, developer or viewer
  levelrail-cli members remove <team> <user-id>            remove a member (the last owner cannot be removed)
  levelrail-cli members leave <team>                       leave a team yourself (signed in sessions only)
```
