# Support

- **Documentation:** [docs.levelrail.com](https://docs.levelrail.com) covers install, configuration, the CLI and the API.
- **Questions and ideas:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/glincker/levelrail/discussions).
- **Bugs and feature requests:** [GitHub issues](https://github.com/glincker/levelrail/issues). Include the version, your install method and the steps to reproduce.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** do not open a public issue. Follow [.github/SECURITY.md](.github/SECURITY.md) and use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting.

Support is provided on a best-effort basis by the maintainer. There is no paid support tier.
